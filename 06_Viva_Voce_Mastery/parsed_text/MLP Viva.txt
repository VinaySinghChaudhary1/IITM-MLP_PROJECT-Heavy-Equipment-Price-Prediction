  1.  Choose  the  number  of  clusters  (K):  You  decide  how  many  groups  you  want.  2.  Place  K  centroids :  Initially,  pick  K  random  points  as  the  center  of  each  cluster.  3. Methods  to  do  hyperparameter  tuning  -  Grid  Search:  Exhaustively  searching  through  a  specified  set  of  hyperparameters.  -  Random  Search:  Randomly  sampling  hyperparameters  from  a  distribution.  -  Bayesian  Optimization:  Using  probabilistic  models  to  guide  the  search  for  the  best
